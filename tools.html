Tool #3 Posture Assessment

Cyber Security Checklist

Check off the essential security habits you follow to measure your personal digital readiness score.

Overall Cyber Readiness: 0% High Exposure

Adds a second verification step to email and banking accounts.

Never reuse credentials across multiple sites; use a password vault.

Apply OS and application patches promptly to close known vulnerabilities.

Verify sender addresses and never click shortened URLs from strangers.

Use a VPN when connecting to airport, hotel, or coffee shop networks.

Store offline or encrypted cloud backups to protect against ransomware.

Enforce biometric or 6+ digit PIN locks on smartphones and laptops.

Audit app permissions and restrict public visibility on social profiles.
