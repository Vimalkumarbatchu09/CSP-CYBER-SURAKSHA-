The Golden Rule of UPI & QR Codes: "Scanning is Only for Paying"

Never scan a QR code or enter your 4-to-6 digit UPI PIN to receive money or lottery winnings. In India's banking system, receiving money requires zero action from your side. Any buyer or stranger asking you to enter a PIN to receive payment is attempting fraud!
