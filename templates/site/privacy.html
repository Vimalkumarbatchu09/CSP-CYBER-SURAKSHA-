Privacy Policy for Cyber Suraksha

Last Updated: November 2026 • Community Service Project

1. Overview

Cyber Suraksha is an educational community service project created by students of Vel Tech Rangarajan Dr. Sagunthala R&D Institute of Science and Technology. Our mission is to raise digital safety and cybercrime awareness among residents of Morai and surrounding communities.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect minimal information necessary to deliver educational tracking and user authentication:

  • Account Information: When you register or sign in via Google OAuth, we receive your name and verified email address to maintain your user account.
  • Educational Progress: Quiz scores, completed scenario modules, and security checklists to help you measure your cybersecurity awareness.

3. How We Use Information

Your information is used strictly for non-commercial educational purposes:

  • To authenticate your access to educational modules.
  • To display your individual learning progress and quiz badges on your personal dashboard.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties or advertisers.

4. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security safeguards including salted Bcrypt password hashing (cost factor 12), HTTPS transport encryption, Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) tokens, and strict anti-caching response headers.

If you have questions about this privacy policy or your data, please contact the project team at vimalkumarbatchu@gmail.com.
