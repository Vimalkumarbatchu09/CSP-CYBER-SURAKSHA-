Security Alert

Think You’ve Been Hacked?

If you suspect unauthorized access to your email, social accounts, or banking credentials, follow these immediate response protocols:

Change Your Password: Reset immediately from a trusted device.
Enable MFA: Block unauthorized logins via two-step verification.
Secure Your Account: Review active login sessions & terminate unknown ones.
Report Activity: File an incident with official cyber helplines (1930 / portal).
