About Cyber Suraksha

Cyber Suraksha is a cybersecurity awareness platform designed to educate users about common cyber threats and provide practical guidance for protecting their digital identity, accounts, devices, and personal information.

Our Three Core Pillars

How We Empower Digital Users

Cyber Awareness

Learn about common cyber threats and online risks. We break down complex attack vectors like phishing emails, ransomware campaigns, and deceptive impersonation scams into clear, easy-to-understand insights.

  • Threat Identification
  • Social Engineering Signals
  • Fake Website Recognition

Digital Protection

Understand how to protect accounts, devices, and personal information. Learn concrete steps to fortify credentials with password managers, enable two-factor authentication, and shield private communications.

  • Multi-Factor Authentication
  • Device Encryption & Passcodes
  • Secure Cloud Backups

Security Guidance

Get simple and practical cybersecurity recommendations. Whether you are browsing on public Wi-Fi, shopping online, or checking bank statements, our guidelines help you prevent attacks proactively.

  • Daily Cyber Hygiene Habits
  • Safe Online Banking Advice
  • Immediate Incident Steps

Our Mission

To democratize cybersecurity knowledge for everyone — from students and non-technical professionals to seniors — by transforming complicated technical concepts into accessible, actionable, and engaging educational experiences.

Our Vision

A digital ecosystem where every individual is equipped with the critical awareness to detect malicious activity, safeguard personal privacy, and navigate the internet with safety and confidence.

Why This Project Matters

The Human Element in Modern Cybersecurity

Over 90% of security incidents originate not from hardware failures or cracked cryptosystems, but from human vulnerabilities — deception, urgency, fear, and fatigue.

The Social Engineering Threat

Attackers now craft hyper-realistic emails and SMS notifications. Training intuition is our strongest defense layer.

Mobile & Digital Banking Surge

With millions adopting instant digital payments and UPI, securing mobile devices and OTPs is no longer optional.
